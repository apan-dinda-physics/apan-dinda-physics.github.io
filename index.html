Research projects

01

Lack of unique steady state in collisional models of cavity–qubit interactions

Ongoing

Studying Jaynes–Cummings qubit–cavity interactions through quantum collisional models, with a mapping to inhomogeneous random walks and deviations from continuous Lindblad dynamics.

02

Engineering Gain via Quantum Measurements

2023 — 2024

Master’s research on a single-qubit gain medium coupled to a bosonic mode, investigating nonlinear behavior, second-order correlations, and Wigner quasiprobability distributions.

03

Dynamics and thermodynamics of a driven resonant level

2023 — 2024

A group study of a resonant level coupled to multiple baths, calculating energy current, transferred heat, and efficiency in the wide-band limit.
